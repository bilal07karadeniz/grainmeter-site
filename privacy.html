Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Nothing is collected

Grainmeter does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The camera

The camera is used only to measure light while the meter is on screen. No photos or video are recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your settings and logbook

Film choice, settings and logbook entries are stored only on your iPhone. They leave it only when you export the logbook yourself.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. Grainmeter never sees your payment details.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@hellobilal.com.
